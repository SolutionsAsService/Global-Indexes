How closely they agree

Position runs from 0 (lowest rank) to 100 (highest rank) for each index, so up and right always mean a higher rank. The figure is a rank correlation (Spearman) over the countries with a record in both indexes. It describes how the two orderings line up; it does not say that one thing causes another, and the two indexes may not measure related things at all.
